## Headline

The POC advanced from a Phase-3 dispatcher in shadow mode to a multi-agent, multi-channel agentic support system. Macro F1 on the 120-case evaluation rose from 0.471 (fallback-only, no LLM) to 0.967 after iterating the prompt, routed against Azure OpenAI `gpt-5.4-mini`. The dispatcher is no longer in shadow: it drives a FAQ specialist for how-to requests, verified before action by a Dual-Model Judge, with a legacy decision engine retained as fallback. Voice and screenshot intake are live as additional channels alongside email. Cost per ticket is measured and dashboard-visible: under one tenth of a US cent per ticket end-to-end.

## Horror-story defenses in place

- Air Canada-style policy invention → FAQ specialist answers only from the retrieved KB article; refusal trigger on miss; signs as "Eva" not as the company.
- Chevy $1 Tahoe via prompt injection → prompt-injection fixtures all route to `escalate` + `requires_human_review`; dispatcher has no tool access.
- Cursor "Sam" hallucinated policy → Dual-Model Judge returns `agrees` and `faithfulness`; downstream honors the verdict. `source` attribution (`model` vs `fallback`) on every dispatch result.
- PHI leak → mandatory redaction before every LLM call; PHI mention forces `requires_human_review=true` regardless of category; screenshot endpoint carries an explicit `phi_likely` flag.

## Risks and blockers

- **Next.js dev hot-reload resets in-memory store.** Affects the dashboard counter between runs; not a production concern because the target deployment persists tickets. Week 4 hardening item is to move the store to SQLite or Postgres.
- **Confidence calibration** — dispatcher reports systematically high confidences (mostly ≥ 0.88). A reliability diagram and threshold tuning remain pending; Week 4 work.
- **Vision deployment capability** — the screenshot endpoint's vision path depends on whether our Azure `gpt-5.4-mini` deployment accepts image inputs. The endpoint falls back cleanly to the `extractedText` path; moving vision to a dedicated `gpt-4o-mini` deployment is the Week 4 option.
- **No proactive detection yet** — Phase 5 of the playbook is untouched. Target a minimum-viable log-hook integration in Week 4.
